01 / ABOUT

Every financial goal
starts with your story.

I'm Nabil Khairi, a Unit Trust & PRS Consultant and Takaful Agent based in Bangi, Selangor.

My client-facing work brings together unit trust and PRS services under Public Mutual Berhad, alongside family and general takaful services.

I aim to make each conversation clear: understand your priorities, explain the relevant options and help you consider the commitments involved.

Alongside these roles, I serve as Administrative Officer for the Jameel Education Foundation UK–USIM Scholarship Programme—a responsibility grounded in care, coordination and accountability.
